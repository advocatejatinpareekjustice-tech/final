Property Document Registration Services

Draft · Verify · Register — Complete End-to-End Assistance in Jaipur

✦

Property transactions and family arrangements require legally sound documents, properly registered at the Sub-Registrar office. Advocate Jatin Pareek provides complete assistance in Jaipur — from drafting the document in correct legal language to completing the registration process without hassle. Each document is carefully prepared to protect your rights and prevent future disputes.

A Sale Deed is the most important document in any property transaction — it legally transfers ownership from the seller to the buyer. Advocate Jatin Pareek prepares the Sale Deed in Jaipur after thoroughly verifying title documents, encumbrance certificates, and existing charges on the property.

The deed is drafted in compliance with the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, and the Registration Act, 1908, and registered at the Sub-Registrar office, Jaipur. Clients are guided through stamp duty calculation, document verification, and Sub-Registrar appointment scheduling.

Key steps handled:
  • Title verification and due diligence
  • Drafting the Sale Deed in proper legal format
  • Stamp duty and registration fee calculation
  • Appointment at Sub-Registrar office, Jaipur
  • Witnessing and registration
  • Delivery of registered copy

A Gift Deed is used to transfer property as a gift — without any monetary consideration — to a family member or any other person. It must be properly drafted, stamped, and registered to be legally valid and binding.

Advocate Jatin Pareek prepares Gift Deeds in Jaipur for parents transferring property to children, between spouses, or in any other gifting situation, and registers them at the Sub-Registrar office after due verification.

Key steps handled:
  • Assessment of applicable stamp duty (reduced for blood relatives under Rajasthan stamp duty rules)
  • Drafting the Gift Deed in correct legal format
  • Registration at Sub-Registrar, Jaipur
  • Advice on tax implications of property gift

A Will is the most personal legal document — it records your wishes for how your property and assets should be distributed after your death. An improperly drafted Will can lead to family disputes and prolonged court battles.

Advocate Jatin Pareek drafts clear, comprehensive Wills in Jaipur that accurately reflect your wishes, naming beneficiaries, executors, and conditions. While registration is not mandatory, it is strongly recommended — and Advocate Pareek guides clients through the process.

Key steps handled:
  • Detailed discussion to understand testator's wishes fully
  • Drafting a clear, unambiguous Will
  • Explaining registration vs. unregistered Will
  • Optional registration at Sub-Registrar office, Jaipur
  • Safe custody advice
  • Guidance on Probate if required later

A Release Deed (Haq-Tyag Patra) is used when a co-owner or legal heir voluntarily gives up their share in a property in favour of another co-owner or heir. This is commonly used in family property settlements and ancestral property divisions in Jaipur.

Advocate Jatin Pareek drafts and registers Release Deeds ensuring all legal formalities are correctly completed, preventing future claims on the released share.

Key steps handled:
  • Understanding the nature of property and parties involved
  • Drafting the Release Deed with proper legal language
  • Stamp duty calculation (based on property value)
  • Registration at Sub-Registrar, Jaipur
  • Guidance on mutation of revenue records after release
  • Agreement to Sell (Bikray Iqrarnama) — Preliminary contract before Sale Deed
  • Mortgage Deed (Rehnnama) — For property-backed loan security
  • Lease Deed (Pattanama) — For long-term lease of commercial/residential property
  • Power of Attorney (Mukhtyarnama) — General & Specific, registered
  • Partition Deed (Baintnama) — Division of joint or ancestral property
  • Affidavits & Declarations — Notarised and Sub-Registrar registered

Land Regularisation — Your Path to Legal Ownership in Jaipur

✦

Thousands of properties and plots in Jaipur exist in areas that developed informally — without full compliance with planning norms. The Rajasthan government has periodically introduced regularisation schemes to bring such properties within the legal framework, providing owners with proper pattas and ownership records.

Advocate Jatin Pareek has guided numerous clients through the complex regularisation process — whether under JDA schemes, Nagar Nigam orders, or State Government notifications. His experience in Revenue Law and civil documentation ensures that clients receive complete, accurate, and timely assistance with their regularisation matters in Jaipur.

Eligibility review JDA / UIT guidance Application filing Hearing support Patta & mutation
1

Document Assessment

We review all your existing documents — original purchase receipts, old agreements, tax receipts, electricity bills, and possession records — to determine eligibility for regularisation under applicable schemes.

2

Scheme Identification

We identify the applicable regularisation scheme — JDA, UIT, Nagar Nigam, Collector, or State Government — based on your plot's location, classification, and cut-off date under the relevant notification.

3

Application Preparation

We prepare the complete regularisation application with all required documents including site plan, ownership proof, affidavits, photographs, and any additional documents required by the relevant authority.

4

Submission and Follow-up

We submit the application to the relevant authority and regularly follow up to track progress, respond to any queries from the authority, and ensure timely processing of your application.

5

Hearing Representation

If a personal hearing is scheduled, Advocate Jatin Pareek personally represents the client before the competent authority — JDA, UIT, Collector — and presents the case effectively to secure a favourable decision.

6

Patta / Certificate Issuance

On approval, we assist with collecting the regularisation certificate or patta from the competent authority and getting mutation done in revenue records so your ownership is fully reflected in official land records.

Revenue Law FAQs

Mutation is the process of updating revenue records (khasra, khatauni) to reflect the new owner's name after a property purchase, inheritance, or gift. It is essential for revenue purposes, government schemes, agricultural loans, and future property transactions. Without mutation, the legal owner's name does not appear in official land records in Rajasthan.

Land Regularisation FAQs

Land regularisation is a legal process by which the Rajasthan government grants formal legal status to plots developed or occupied outside approved planning frameworks. The Rajasthan government periodically launches regularisation schemes. Availability depends on current government notifications.
